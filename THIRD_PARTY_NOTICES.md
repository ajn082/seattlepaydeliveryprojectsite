# Third-party notices

Source Serif 4 and IBM Plex Sans are self-hosted from their Fontsource packages, with the supplied font files redistributed unmodified. Both use the SIL Open Font License 1.1. License texts supplied by the installed packages are preserved in:

- `public/licenses/Source-Serif-4-OFL.txt`
- `public/licenses/IBM-Plex-Sans-OFL.txt`

The static build copies those notices to `dist/licenses/`. The original typographic favicon, social artwork, and time schematic are part of this project. No reference-site artwork or photographs are copied.

Build dependencies retain their own licenses in their installed packages. The source archive includes the package manifest and lockfile so that dependencies can be installed reproducibly.

GSAP, ScrollTrigger, and SplitText are bundled locally for the story animations under the standard license declared by their package. See `public/licenses/GSAP-NOTICE.txt` for the copyright notice and license URL. No third-party animation CDN is used.
